Privacy Policy

StudyGate · Last updated: October 7, 2026

StudyGate helps you turn study material into structured study guides, quizzes, and flashcards. This policy explains what we handle when you use the app, and what we deliberately leave alone.

The short version

We do not require you to create an account. We do not sell your data. We do not use your study material to train AI models. Study material you paste or import stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.

1 · What we collect

WhatWhyWhere it lives
Study material you paste or import (text, PDF contents) Processed locally to build your guide. Sent to our server only when you tap Generate. On device + our server, transiently
Generated study guides, quiz items, and your progress state Core functionality — this is the product. On device
Anonymous install identifier Enforcing the free-tier limit (5 guides) and preventing abuse. Our server
Subscription status and transaction receipts Confirming your Pro access. Apple / our server
Crash logs and diagnostic info Finding and fixing bugs. Our server

We do not collect: your name, email, contacts, photos (beyond materials you explicitly import), precise location, or browsing history.

2 · How we use your material

Your material is yours

We use it for one thing: generating your study guide. We do not use your material to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model. We do not sell it. We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Generated guides are deleted from our servers within a short retention window after generation.

3 · Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, with these categories of recipients:

  • Apple — for App Store purchases, receipts, and subscription management. Apple does not give us your payment card details.
  • Our AI infrastructure providers — subprocessors that run the language model on your behalf, under contract, with instructions not to retain your material. This is the only time study material leaves our control, and only to produce your guide.
  • Analytics and crash reporting providers — in aggregated form, containing no study content.
  • Professional advisors or authorities — where required by law.

We are not a party to any arrangement between you and these providers beyond the processing described here.

4 · Where your data goes

Study material and generated guides are stored on your device. Limited technical data (anonymized install ID, subscription status, crash logs) is processed on servers located in the United States.

5 · How long we keep things

ON DEVICE Study material and generated guides stay until you delete them. Clearing cached material in Settings removes the stored source text. Deleting the app removes everything.

SERVER Transient processing data is discarded shortly after generation. Anonymized aggregate statistics and crash logs are retained for as long as needed to operate and improve the service.

6 · Your controls

  • Delete material — Settings → Clear cached material, or delete individual sessions from History.
  • Delete everything — delete the app from your device.
  • Manage your subscription — through your App Store account settings.
  • Ask what's stored — email us and we'll tell you, and delete it if you want.

7 · Children's privacy

StudyGate is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us material or personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

8 · Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email us. We respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9 · Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to production systems is restricted to people who need it and protected by multi-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we don't claim otherwise.

10 · Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use StudyGate after a change means you accept the revised policy.
